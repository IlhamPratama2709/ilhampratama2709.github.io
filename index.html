Bachelor's thesis · 2026

Spillover or Still Over? Do Indonesia's SEZs Leave Light and Environmental Signatures Beyond Their Borders?

Across 514 districts, no type of zone raised night-time light nearby. Tourism zones raised CO₂ by about 4.5 percent.
